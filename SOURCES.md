## 1. Primary instruments located and verified

### 1.1 Insurance Act 1978 (consolidated)
- **Exact title:** Insurance Act 1978 (1978 : 39), official consolidation.
- **Issuing authority:** Bermuda legislature; consolidation published by the Government of Bermuda (bermudalaws.bm).
- **Consolidation currency:** text includes amendments through the **Insurance Amendment (No. 2) Act 2025 (2025 : 33), effective 7 January 2026** (per section annotations, e.g. ss.1, 6C, 18A, 30CA, 32D "amended by 2025 : 33 … effective 7 January 2026").
- **Source URL:** https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1978/Insurance%20Act%201978 (PDF, 143 pp.)
- **Retrieved:** 2 July 2026. Full text downloaded and searched; provisions below quoted from the retrieved text.
- **Used for / provisions verified verbatim:**
  - **s.3(1)** — prohibition on carrying on insurance business in or from within Bermuda unless registered under s.4.
  - **s.8 / s.8A** — principal office & principal representative requirement; principal representative must "forthwith notify" the Authority of specified events (incl. likelihood of insolvency, significant loss likely to breach ECR, criminal proceedings, ceasing business, class-limit breaches) and file a written report **within 14 days**; wilful failure to give required notice is an offence; 45-day post-loss CSR (s.8A(2a)) and 30-day interim statements (s.8A(2b)) follow-ups.
  - **s.14(2)** — annual fee payable **before 31 March** in every year following registration, in the amount prescribed under the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969; **s.14(3)** — late penalty fee of **10% of the fee due for every month or part month** unpaid; s.14(5) quarterly remission of registration fees (25/50/75%).
  - **s.17(1)/(3)/(4)** — duty to keep and file statutory financial statements; **"filing date"**: Classes 1, 2, 3 (not also C/D/E), A, B — **six months** after financial year end "(or such longer period, not exceeding **nine months**, as the Authority may allow … on an application made to it for that purpose)"; Classes 3A, IIGB, Collateralized Insurer, 3B, SPI, 4, C, IILT, D, E — **four months** "(or such longer period, not exceeding **seven months**, as the Authority may allow … on an application …)".
  - **s.17A(5)** — additional GAAP financial statements (Class 3A, 3B, 4, C, D, E) filed within **four months**, extendable to not more than **seven months**.
  - **s.18(1)** — statutory financial return filed at the same time as the statutory financial statements.
  - **s.18A(1)–(2)** — late fees for failure to file statements/returns/CSR: per **week or part week**, up to **$500** (Class 1/2/3/A/B, managers, brokers, agents, marketplace providers), **$1,000** (Class 3A, IIGB, Collateralized, SPI, IILT, C, D), **$5,000** (Class 3B, 4, E); **s.18A(5)** — Authority may appoint an inspector under s.30 where filings remain outstanding **three months** past the filing date.
  - **s.30D / 30E / 30EA / 30F / 30G** — shareholder controller regime: private companies — prior written notice and 45-day no-objection for becoming a **10%, 20%, 33% or 50%** shareholder controller; public companies — notice within 45 days after; disposal notifications; objection powers; **s.30G offences**: summary conviction fine **$25,000** (failure to notify), and for continuing as a controller after a notice of objection an **additional $500 per day**; on indictment **$100,000 or 2 years' imprisonment or both**.
  - **s.30JEA** — statutory duty on insurers to **forthwith notify** the Authority of a cyber reporting event and file a written report **within 14 days** (definition of "cyber reporting event" included).
  - **s.31A** — failure to meet the **minimum margin of solvency**: immediate notification, 14-day written report with rectification plan, and **no dividends until the failure is rectified**.
  - **s.31AA** — failure to comply with the **enhanced capital requirement**: immediate notification, 14-day report/plan, **45-day** unaudited EBS + interim GAAP statements etc., dividend freeze.
  - **s.31B** — dividends exceeding **25% of total statutory capital and surplus** (commercial classes): affidavit signed by two directors and the principal representative filed **at least 7 days before payment**; further restrictions where relevant margins not met (s.31B(3)-(4)).
  - **s.31C** — prior BMA approval required before reducing **total statutory capital by 15% or more** (commercial + captive classes per subsections); Collateralized Insurers: 30-day post notification.
  - **s.32** — powers of intervention: directions (incl. cease writing, premium caps, no variations increasing liabilities …) on grounds incl. breach of the Act/Rules, ECR breach, unapproved controller.
  - **s.32D** — civil penalty up to **$500,000** per failure/contravention ("effective, proportionate and dissuasive"); due-diligence defence; carve-outs for ss.14(3), 15A(6), 18A, 30CA(4); **s.32E** warning/decision notice procedure.
  - **s.32F** — public censure of a registered person; **s.32H–32J** — prohibition orders against individuals (breach: summary **$50,000**/2 years; indictment **$200,000**/4 years).
  - **s.41 / s.42 / s.44** — cancellation of registration grounds (false/misleading information, unsound business conduct, minimum criteria) and gazetting.
  - **s.50 (Issue of false documents)** — offence of issuing false/misleading documents for the purposes of the Act.
  - **s.29B, s.30, s.30A–30C** — information/report powers, investigations, obstruction offences ($50,000/2 yrs summary; $200,000/5 yrs indictment for document destruction).
- Also located: BMA-hosted consolidation (older upload): https://cdn.bma.bm/documents/2019-03-27-09-23-29-Insurance-Act-1978.pdf and http://www.commonlii.org/bm/legis/consol_act/ia1978116.pdf (not used — superseded by bermudalaws.bm text).

### 1.2 2026 BMA fee schedule
- **Exact title:** "Bermuda Monetary Authority Fees Effective 1 January 2026" — summary of fees pursuant to the **Fourth Schedule of the Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969**.
- **Issuing authority:** Bermuda Monetary Authority. **Effective:** 1 January 2026 (document uploaded 17 March 2026).
- **Source URL:** https://cdn.bma.bm/documents/2026-03-17-10-11-27-2026-Bermuda-Monetary-Authority-Fees.pdf (via https://www.bma.bm/fees)
- **Retrieved:** 2 July 2026. Full 30-page PDF downloaded and extracted.
- **Fee data captured (Insurance Act section of the schedule):**

  - **Filing-deadline extension under s.17(4)** — per month past the filing deadline: Class IIGB/3A/C/IILT/D **$1,500 per month** (first/second/third month); Class 3B/4/E **$2,500 per month** (first/second/third month); Class 1/2/3/A/B, Collateralized, SPI **$750 per month** (first/second/third month). *The schedule prices no month beyond the third — consistent with the statutory 3-month extension cap in s.17(4).* One fee where multiple filings extended together (s.17(4)/s.6C).
  - BSCR filing extension under s.6C: $1,500/month (both class groups, three months priced). CISSA/GAAP/FCR extensions under s.6C: $1,500/month (three months priced).
  - s.30D controller notification: **$750**. s.31B dividend affidavit: **$550**. s.31C capital reduction approval: **$1,500**. s.56 direction: **$1,000** (specified categories also $1,000). s.57A direction: $2,500. s.6C/27FA(1) prudential-standard exemption/modification: **$1,000**; quarterly-return exemption/modification: $2,500.
  - **Internal capital model**: pre-application $55,000; review & approval min **$200,000** – max **$2,000,000** (sliding scale); annual monitoring **$30,000**; major-change post-approval $30,000.
  - **Scenario-based approach (SBA) model** (Rules under s.6A/27F): review & approval — one-time, case-by-case, min **$120,000** – max **$1,500,000**; **annual monitoring $250,000**; limited-basis assets in BEL min $10,000–max $500,000 (subsequent $10,000); long-duration assets same; insurer-specific default cost assumptions $15,000 (re-approval **every two years**, $10,000).

Run-off tiers also captured for 3A/3B/4.
  - **Group fees (s.27B, item 8)**: group supervision annual fee $211,700 / $279,000 / $545,900 by GPW or asset tier; IAIG fees $1,000,000+ (long-term) and $545,900 + 0.002% GWP capped $1,249,000 (general).
  - Annual fee billing basis: assigned class as at 1 January 2026; pro-rata on cancellation before 31 March; statutory penalty fees per s.14(3).

### 1.12 Filing-extension limit (research directive)
- **User hypothesis tested:** "after a third granted filing-extension request no further extension is available; continued non-filing is automatically a breach."
- **What the sources support:** the limit is a **statutory cap on the aggregate extended period**, not a count of requests: s.17(4) permits the Authority to allow "such longer period, **not exceeding seven months**" (four-month classes) or "**not exceeding nine months**" (six-month classes) — i.e. a maximum of **three additional months** in each case. The 2026 fee schedule (bma-primary) prices extension applications only for the **first, second and third months** past the filing deadline — no fourth month exists in the schedule, consistent with the statutory cap. Once the capped period expires, the Authority has **no power under s.17(4)** to allow more time; continued non-filing is a contravention attracting s.18A weekly late fees, possible inspector appointment (s.18A(5)), s.32 intervention and s.32D civil penalties.
- **Encoded as:** statutory cap [ESTABLISHED — Act text]; per-month fee structure [VERIFY — 2026 schedule]; "three requests then automatic breach" framing corrected to "maximum three months' aggregate extension".
